# Proton Gradient and ATP Synthase

Subject: Biology

ATP synthesis depends on the proton gradient, and the proton gradient depends
on the electron transport chain. That is the chain of dependency to remember.

The electron transport chain pumps protons to one side of a membrane. This
creates a high concentration of protons on that side and a charge difference
across the membrane.

Protons flow back down their electrochemical gradient through ATP synthase.
ATP synthase is a rotary enzyme: the flow of protons physically turns part of
the enzyme, and that rotation drives the reaction that joins ADP and phosphate
into ATP.

This process is called chemiosmosis and it is identical in mitochondria and
chloroplasts.

Because ATP synthesis requires the proton gradient, and the gradient requires
the electron transport chain, and the chain requires redox reactions, a gap
anywhere earlier in that chain shows up as confusion here.
