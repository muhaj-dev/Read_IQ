# Enzymes and RuBisCO

Subject: Biology

Enzymes lower activation energy. RuBisCO is the enzyme that fixes carbon
dioxide in the Calvin cycle, and it is often called the most abundant protein
on Earth.

Understanding enzyme kinetics is a prerequisite for understanding why RuBisCO
is inefficient. RuBisCO sometimes binds oxygen instead of carbon dioxide,
which causes photorespiration and wastes energy.

Because the Calvin cycle depends on RuBisCO, and RuBisCO depends on enzyme
behaviour, enzyme kinetics sits upstream of the Calvin cycle.
