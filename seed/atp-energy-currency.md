# ATP — The Energy Currency

Subject: Biology

ATP is the molecule cells use to move energy around. Understanding ATP depends
on first understanding the proton gradient and ATP synthase.

ATP synthase is a rotary enzyme driven by a proton gradient. The gradient is
built by the electron transport chain in both photosynthesis and cellular
respiration.

The Calvin cycle consumes ATP. The Krebs cycle helps produce it. Any question
about either topic depends on knowing how ATP is made.
