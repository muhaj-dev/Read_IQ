# electrochemical gradient

Subject: Chemistry

An electrochemical gradient is a difference in both concentration and charge
across a membrane. Understanding it requires understanding redox reactions,
because in cells the energy to build the gradient comes from electron transfer.

Two things drive movement across a membrane: the concentration difference of a
species, and the electrical difference caused by charge. Together these form
the electrochemical gradient.

Ions moving down their gradient release energy. Cells capture that energy and
use it to do work. This is called chemiosmosis.

The proton gradient in mitochondria and chloroplasts is an electrochemical
gradient. So understanding this topic is a prerequisite for understanding how
ATP synthase works at all.
