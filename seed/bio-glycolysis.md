# Glycolysis

Subject: Biology

Glycolysis is the first stage of respiration and it happens in the cytoplasm.
It does not require oxygen.

One glucose molecule is split into two pyruvate molecules. The net yield is
two ATP and two NADH.

Glycolysis produces NADH, and NADH carries electrons to the electron transport
chain. So glycolysis feeds the chain rather than depending on it.

Glycolysis must be understood before the Krebs cycle, because pyruvate is what
the Krebs cycle starts from. Pyruvate moves into the mitochondrion and is
converted into acetyl-CoA.
